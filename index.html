Picking (Instrument panel) chooses what plucks the strings: a pick, bare fingertips, short or long nails, or pick and fingers together. With fingers the thumb takes the bass strings; accented notes are played as rest strokes.

Body model: Modelled is the instrument computed from its wood, bracing and air, with the strings and body feeding each other through the bridge; it takes a couple of seconds the first time. Classic is the earlier hand-tuned body. String coupling sets how strongly the strings feel the bridge and each other through it. 100 % is the value computed from the model; there, notes on the body's main resonance (around F♯3–G3) behave like the wolf notes of a real guitar.

Techniques combine: a palm-muted note can also be accented, hammered or bent.

Notes ring until the same string is played again or stopped with . — just like a real guitar. Notes in the same column are strummed. Your song lives in the page address: copy it any time to share.
